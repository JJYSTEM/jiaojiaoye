<h2 id="-research-interest">🚀 Research interest</h2>

<p>My research wish is to build a robust, fully automated system 🤖 with learning-based methods in the challenging and uncertain real-world. Particularly, I am keen to conduct research in the interdisciplinary area of Embodied Agents and Artificial Intelligence, with a passion for precise robotic perception and decision-making in a 3D complicated and non-stationary environment.</p>

<p>My research interest is located in the following areas:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Generative Model</li>
  <li>Embodied AI</li>
  <li>World Model Learning</li>
</ul>

<p>My Ph.D. work builds on three closely-related questions:</p>
<ol>
  <li>How can we efficiently acquire massive, high-quality datasets for scaling foundation models? I explore a training-free generation pipeline that utilizes pre-trained diffusion-based generative models alongside VLMs to synthesize large-scale, well-annotated datasets without incurring additional training costs.</li>
  <li>How can we enhance a model's reasoning, planning, and memory capabilities to ensure robust performance in dynamic environments, particularly when addressing complex, long-horizon tasks?</li>
  <li>How can we optimize a robotic system's computational efficiency and reduce its processing burden to boost real-time execution in the physical world?</li>
</ol>
